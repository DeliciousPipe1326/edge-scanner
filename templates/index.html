Understanding Middles: Middles are high-variance plays. They typically lose small amounts and win big when the final score lands inside the gap. No middle probability or EV is predicted without a validated outcome distribution. Break-even probability is the frequency of winning both bets required to cover the worst non-middle payoff. Payoff scenarios include whole-line pushes. First and last scenario rows represent the outside tails.

Understanding +EV Betting: Sharp books (Pinnacle, Betfair, Matchbook) provide reference prices; removing vig does not establish true probabilities. A higher soft-book price implies positive EV under that reference model. Win rates depend on the odds, and estimated edges can be wrong. Kelly staking helps size bets, but variance can still be high. Always confirm odds before betting; edges disappear quickly.
